988 Survey Explorer

Two ways into the same 1,255 post-training survey responses. Click anything to narrow the view; the panel beside it always describes what you have selected.

Measure

Where the training has reached

Every Maryland county is drawn. Four have survey responses and can be clicked; the other twenty are grey because the training has not been surveyed there.

Inside the selected area

Pick a county on the map, then split it whichever way you like.

Reading the map

  • Colour shows the measure chosen above; grey means no responses at all.
  • A figure is shown for a group of 30 or more. For 10 to 29 it is marked as a small sample. Below 10 the count is shown but no figure, because a number from so few people misleads and could identify someone.
  • "Would call 988" was only on some versions of the survey, so a large county can still have no figure for it.
Rings

Every response, split and split again

Each wedge is a group of responses, sized by how many people are in it. Click a wedge to go deeper; click the middle to come back out.

Reading the rings

  • The middle is everyone. The first ring splits them by the feature you chose above, the second ring splits each of those by the other feature, and the outer ring splits those by training session.
  • A wedge's size is how many people it holds; its colour is the measure chosen above. A striped wedge has fewer than 10 people, so no figure is shown for it.
  • The same groups appear whichever ring order you choose; only the nesting changes.
