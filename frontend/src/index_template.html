What the numbers are

    What they are not

    • Not causal. Before-scores are recalled after the training on the same form, so gains include how people now remember their old selves.
    • Not a Maryland-wide picture. Four of 24 jurisdictions have responses, and Anne Arundel students dominate the set.
    • Open-text themes are keyword-coded, so a theme share is a floor, not a prevalence estimate.
    • Responses cluster inside training sessions (ICC ≈ 0.20), so the 95% intervals shown in the explorer are roughly 2.3× too narrow.

    Cleaning decisions

      Built from the EveryMind BuildFest analysis tables (gemini_kit/data/) and the 988 Survey Explorer (frontend/explorer/). Rebuild with python frontend/build_site.py --explorer.

      Survey responses are de-identified and aggregated. No group smaller than 10 people is given a figure anywhere on this page or in the explorer.
