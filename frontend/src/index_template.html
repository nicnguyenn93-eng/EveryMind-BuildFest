Method and limits

How to read all of this

What the numbers are

  • Counts, agreement, NPS and gain come from one cleaned, censored export.
  • NPS is the share rating 9–10 minus the share rating 0–6; it is not the mean score.
  • The knowledge gain index is the change in mean before/after items on a 1–5 scale.
  • Groups under 10 responses are withheld; 10–29 is a small sample.

What they are not

  • Not causal. Before-scores are recalled after the training on the same form.
  • A convenience sample, not Maryland residents overall. The map covers Maryland; totals include other surveyed locations.
  • Open-text interpretations stay draft until staff review them.
  • NPS intervals, correlations and trends are not calculated here.

Cleaning decisions

  • Every retained row is included; quality flags do not exclude records.
  • Cleaning and censoring run on your computer before any figure is made.
