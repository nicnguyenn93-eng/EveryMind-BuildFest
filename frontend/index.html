What this is

A reading of the post-training survey, built in your browser

EveryMind runs Understanding 988 trainings across Maryland and asks everyone who attends the same short survey. This tool turns those answers into the things a programme team can act on: what the training moved, where it did not, and what to change next — with the evidence under every claim and the interactive county map underneath. Load the export and it builds.

What it reads

  • The survey export exactly as the survey tool produces it — no manual tidying first.
  • Three before/after knowledge items, the presenter ratings, the recommend and call-988 scores, and the two open-text boxes.
  • Everything happens in this browser. Nothing is uploaded, and the page never contacts a server.

What it checks

  • Whether the file is readable, how many responses it holds, and how many columns it has.
  • Whether all 20 columns the analysis pipeline expects are present, naming any that are missing.
  • A SHA-256 of the file, computed locally, so a run can be tied to an exact export.

What you get

  • Headline figures for the whole survey, then six findings, each with its own chart.
  • Seven recommendations, each with its evidence, an owner, and the measure that would show whether it worked.
  • The interactive Maryland map, to go county by county yourself.

What happens to your file: it is parsed in the page with the browser's own file reader to count rows and check column names. The figures in the report come from the pre-computed aggregate tables built into this page, not from your upload — running the cleaning, redaction and analysis pipeline on a new export is a job for python -m everymind_cleaning.run_pipeline, not for a browser.

What the numbers are

    What they are not

    • Not causal. Before-scores are recalled after the training on the same form, so gains include how people now remember their old selves.
    • Not a Maryland-wide picture. Four of 24 jurisdictions have responses, and Anne Arundel students dominate the set.
    • Open-text themes are keyword-coded, so a theme share is a floor, not a prevalence estimate.
    • Responses cluster inside training sessions (ICC ≈ 0.20), so the 95% intervals shown in the explorer are roughly 2.3× too narrow.

    Cleaning decisions

      Built from the EveryMind BuildFest analysis tables (gemini_kit/data/) and the 988 Survey Explorer (frontend/explorer/). Rebuild with python frontend/build_site.py --explorer.

      Survey responses are de-identified and aggregated. No group smaller than 10 people is given a figure anywhere on this page or in the explorer.
