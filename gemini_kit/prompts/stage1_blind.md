# Task: independent analysis

Below are the aggregate tables from the post-training surveys. Each table has a name, a short description and its rows in CSV form.

You have **not** been shown any other analyst's conclusions. Work from the tables alone.

Produce:

1. **summary**: four to six sentences a program manager could read in thirty seconds.
2. **findings**: the 6 to 10 most important conclusions, ordered by importance. Cover each of these at least once:
   - how effective the training is (what changed, for whom, and how much to trust the size of the change);
   - knowledge gaps (what participants still don't know or can't do);
   - behavioral-intent gaps (where knowing does not translate into intending to act);
   - differences between participant groups (role, county, language), separating real differences from composition effects;
   - what the open-answer themes reveal;
   - data-quality or measurement problems that limit any conclusion.
   For each finding, link it to a psychological or evaluation framework if one genuinely explains it.
3. **recommendations**: 5 to 8 actions, ordered by expected impact relative to effort.
4. **predictions**: 2 to 4 program-level forecasts for the next two quarters, with uncertainty and the condition that would change each one.
5. **data_limitations**: what this data cannot tell us.
6. **new_survey_questions**: up to 6 questions EveryMind should add to the survey, each with the gap it would explain.

Before writing, look for at least one pattern that a quick read of the headline numbers would miss (for example, a composition effect, a ceiling effect, or a subgroup that behaves differently).
